The Delivery Model

When go-live deadlines are tight, you need an independent, senior technical partner who takes total accountability for the output without requiring hand-holding or internal supervision.

Outcome-Based Delivery

You define the required output schema; we take full responsibility for building and executing the extraction, cleansing, and staging pipelines to deliver clean data.

Senior Principal Execution

Direct technical execution from initial scoping to final deployment. No junior delegation, hand-offs, or management layer overhead—your project is handled by an engineer with 30 years of delivery experience.

Direct B2B Corporate Contract

Engagements are contracted directly through Shim Systems Pty Ltd as a standard B2B vendor contract, avoiding payroll tax, superannuation, or internal administrative friction.
